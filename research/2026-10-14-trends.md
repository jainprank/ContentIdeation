# Trend report: #dancefitness, 2026-10-14

**Source:** data-slayer/instagram-hashtag-posts-reels-scraper-no-login, basic mode, 30 items, run 2026-10-14. Public data only, no login.
**Caveat:** this is one hashtag and 30 posts. Treat it as a first read, not a trend forecast. Audio names were not pulled in this run; the scraper returns audio metadata, so the next run should include it.

## Summary
- Most of the top posts are reels (26 of 30). Carousels and images appear but get far fewer plays.
- Top plays cluster in two groups: short, punchy clips (under 20 s) and longer 60–90 s choreography.
- Zumba and belly-dance class posts are common among the high-play posts. Audio for them was not checked in this run.
- Several top posts are instructor or studio content, not personal daily-life content. That gap is an opportunity for a fun, real, two-person page.
- Some tags in this set lean toward weight-loss messaging (#WeightLossJourney, #FatLossTips). Our brand avoids that, so we skip those tags.
